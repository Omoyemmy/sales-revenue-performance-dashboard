# Sales Revenue Performance Dashboard

An Excel dashboard analyzing sales and revenue performance using data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables, charts, KPIs, and slicers.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to evaluate revenue, profitability, product performance, geographic performance, and revenue trends over time.

The dashboard was developed in Microsoft Excel using data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables, charts, KPIs, and interactive slicers to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## Key Insights

* The business generated **$22.34 million in total revenue** and **$2.26 million in total profit**, resulting in an overall profit margin of approximately **10%**.

* **Bikes generated the highest revenue** among the three product categories, followed by **Accessories** and **Clothing**.

* Most products were classified as **Medium performance (56.26%)**, while **34.88%** were classified as Low performance and only **8.86%** as High performance.

* The **United States generated the highest revenue** among the countries analyzed, followed by **Germany, the United Kingdom, and France**.

* **California generated the highest revenue** among the top five states shown, followed by **England, Washington, Oregon, and Saarland**.
  
* Profit was negative from January to June 2015, with the lowest monthly profit occurring in June 2015 at -$8,691. Profit turned positive in July 2015, indicating an improvement in profitability after the first six months.

* **June 2016 recorded the highest revenue**, while **January 2015 recorded the lowest revenue**
